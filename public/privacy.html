Privacy notice

Last updated 12 October 2026

This document is a plain-language statement of how the service works today. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

GovernancePro Global (“we”) runs this website: a self-assessment and consulting-enquiry platform for ISO, IT governance, risk and compliance readiness. This notice is written with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in mind and is meant to be readable by visitors from anywhere. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we will handle your requests in line with the law that applies to you.

What we collect

  • Assessment form: your name, company, email, optional telephone number, the framework you chose, your organisation size, your answers about documentation, management support, timeline and the help you need, an optional description of your biggest challenge, your region, and a record that you agreed to be contacted (the time, and the version of the wording shown).
  • Waitlist and enquiry forms: your name, email, optional organisation, what you are interested in, an optional message, and the same record of agreement.
  • Cost calculator: the options you choose (framework, service, size, maturity, region). No contact details are collected by the calculator, so this record is anonymous.
  • Derived results: the maturity score, band and indicative cost range we calculate from what you entered.
  • Technical data: your network address is used to limit abusive and repeated requests. It is stored only as a salted hash in short-lived counters that are cleared within about a day, and it is not added to your record.

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics on this site. We do not collect payment card details.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To give you your result and reply to your request (you asked us to).
  • To contact you about the assessment, a waitlist you joined, or consulting you asked about (with your consent, which you can withdraw).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (our legitimate interest).

We do not sell personal information.

Who sees it

Only authorised GovernancePro Global administrators can view assessment and enquiry records. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which stores and processes the data on our behalf and may do so outside South Africa. If we ever refer you to a consulting partner, we will ask you first and tell you who it is.

How long we keep it

We keep records only as long as they are needed to deal with your request, review them at least once a year, and delete those no longer needed. Anonymous calculator records contain no personal information. You can ask us to delete your record sooner.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to withdraw your consent to be contacted. Use the request form on the SaaS Solutions page and say that your message is a privacy request, and we will respond. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa), or to the data-protection authority where you live.

Security

Records are readable only after administrator authentication; wrong administrator passwords and repeated submissions are rate-limited; scores and estimates are calculated on the server; and the site sends browser security headers. No system is perfectly secure, and this service has not had an independent security audit.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change and show the date at the top.
