The problem

Most organisations preparing for ISO 27001, ISO 9001, ISO 20000 or a governance review start without knowing how ready they are, what the gaps cost to close, or where to begin. Consultants quote before the problem is understood, and certification deadlines arrive anyway.

  • A first, structured view of readiness.
  • A plain-language next step, not a 200-line checklist.
  • A planning range for the cost of getting help, in your own currency.
  • A route to a specialist when you want one.

What works today

Everything in this list is live on this site now. It is free and needs no account.

Readiness self-assessment

Choose from nine frameworks: ISO 27001, ISO 9001, ISO 22301, ISO 20000, ISO 45001, ITSM, COBIT, IT Governance and GRC. Answer a short set of questions.

Security and data handling

What we can say because it is built and has been tested:

  • Lead and assessment records can be read only by an authenticated administrator; the administration area and its data interfaces refuse everyone else.
  • Administrator sign-in attempts with a wrong password are limited.
  • Your score and any estimate are calculated on our server from your answers, not taken from your browser.
  • Your agreement to be contacted is recorded with the time and the version of the wording shown.
  • Repeated and automated submissions are limited.
  • Data is stored in Cloudflare’s database service. We do not collect payment card details.

What we cannot claim

  • No independent security audit or certification yet.
  • No customer accounts yet, so no single sign-on or two-factor sign-in.
  • No data-residency region has been configured.

Frequently asked questions

Is the assessment an audit or a certification?+

No. It is an indicative self-assessment based on your own answers. It is not an independent audit, accredited certification or legal advice, and it does not guarantee a certification outcome.

Can I buy a subscription or a report today?+

No. Subscription plans and paid readiness reports are proposals that are still being built. You can join the waitlist; we do not take payment details and there is no obligation.

What is free today?+

The readiness self-assessment, the indicative maturity result with a recommended next step, and the consulting cost calculator. No account is needed.

Are the prices final?+

No. They are proposed prices and may change before plans open. We will confirm local-currency prices at launch.

How accurate are the cost ranges?+

They are planning ranges only. Final pricing depends on confirmed scope, locations, complexity, documentation and delivery requirements. Amounts in other currencies use fixed, indicative conversion rates, not live exchange rates.

Who can see my assessment?+

Authorised GovernancePro Global administrators only. We only contact you if you agreed to it. You can ask us to delete your record at any time.

Do you provide consulting?+

We take consulting enquiries and can connect you with a suitable specialist. Consulting is a separate engagement with its own agreement.

Contact and support
