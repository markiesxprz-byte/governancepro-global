# GovernancePro Global — 90-day launch plan

Targets below are **planning targets to revise after four weeks of real data**, not forecasts or promises. Nothing here
has been launched; no campaign, list or message has been sent.

## 1. Positioning (use exactly this, do not stretch it)
**GovernancePro Global helps organisations see how ready they are for ISO and IT-governance standards, understand the gaps,
and decide what to do next, starting with a free self-assessment.**

**Can say:** free self-assessment across nine frameworks; indicative score, band and next step; an indicative consulting
cost range; consulting enquiries welcome; SaaS plans and paid reports are on a waitlist.
**Must not say:** audit, certification, "compliant", guaranteed outcomes, "trusted by", testimonials, that SaaS plans or
reports can be bought, or any feature marked Planned as if it exists.

## 2. Target customers
| Profile | Why they care | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| Small and mid-size companies preparing for ISO 27001 (customer or tender requirement) | Need a starting point and a cost range before approaching a consultant | LinkedIn, tender-driven sectors, SA and wider EMEA |
| IT and operations managers wanting an ITSM/ISO 20000 or COBIT maturity view | Need to justify a programme internally | LinkedIn, ITSM and governance communities |
| Independent consultants and small advisory firms | Could use the assessment as a lead-in tool (future MSP/Partner plan) | Professional networks |

## 5. Weekly rhythm (planning targets)
- 20 personalised LinkedIn connection notes to the profiles above.
- Reply to every conversation within one business day.
- 2 short calls booked from those conversations.
- 1 content piece (see ideas below), reused as a LinkedIn post.
- Review every New lead in the admin screen.

## 6. Messages (adapt, never mass-send)
**LinkedIn connection note:** "Hi {name}, I run GovernancePro Global. We built a free ISO and IT-governance readiness self-assessment that gives an indicative score and next step in a few minutes. Happy to share it if it's useful for {company}."
**After they accept:** "Thanks for connecting. If {ISO 27001 / ISO 20000 / COBIT} is on your agenda, the self-assessment is here: {link}. It's indicative only, not an audit. If the result raises questions, I'm glad to talk it through."
**Follow-up after the assessment (manual; automated email is not built):** "Thanks for completing the assessment. Your result was {score}/100 ({band}). The suggested next step is {recommendation}. Want a 20-minute call to talk through what that would involve? No obligation."
**Gentle follow-up (7 days):** "Just checking whether the assessment raised anything you'd like to talk through. If now isn't the time, no problem."
Rules: no more than two follow-ups; stop if asked; keep a record of who asked to stop.

## 7. Walkthrough script (10 minutes)
1. Why readiness comes before consulting spend. 2. Pick the framework that matches their goal. 3. Answer the four questions together. 4. Show the score, band and next step, and state clearly it is indicative. 5. Show the cost range and explain the fixed indicative conversion for their currency. 6. Ask what would help most: a conversation, a waitlist place for the report or subscription, or nothing yet.

## 8. Content ideas
"ISO 27001 readiness: five questions to ask before you hire a consultant"; "What does ISO certification preparation actually cost (and why ranges, not quotes)"; "ISO 20000 vs ITIL: what changes for a small IT team"; "How to read a maturity score"; "A checklist for your first management-review meeting".

## 9. 90-day checklist
**Days 1-14 — foundation:** back up the database; deploy v3.1 (approval required); register `RATE_LIMIT_SALT`; confirm the privacy notice and terms have had legal review; publish a monitored contact address; confirm `/admin` is protected; test the forms yourself; decide who answers leads and how fast.
**Days 15-45 — first conversations:** start the weekly rhythm; review New leads every other day; record outcomes; write two content pieces; check search-console indexing for `/`, `/saas`, `/privacy`, `/terms`.
**Days 46-90 — learn and decide:** review conversion at each pipeline stage; ask waitlist members what Starter should include **before** building it; decide whether paid reports are worth building (and how delivery and payment would work); only then scope accounts and payments.

## 10. Metrics (read from the admin screen and CSV export)
Visits (from Cloudflare analytics if enabled) → assessments started and completed → leads with consent → calls → proposals → won; waitlist sign-ups by interest; time to first reply. **Revenue is not tracked in the software**; record won consulting engagements and payments yourself, separately from forecasts.

## 11. Compliance reminders
Direct electronic marketing is regulated in South Africa (POPIA) and elsewhere. Use consent, honour opt-outs, and get legal advice before any bulk or cold email. Do not claim legal compliance or certification without review.
